# Little Lotus

Quizzes, talks and printable worksheets from Shri Mataji Nirmala Devi's own words, for every age from 1 to 26+.

## Download for Android

1. On your Android phone (Android 7 or newer), open the [latest release](https://github.com/sahajisy/little-lotus/releases/latest) and tap **LittleLotus.apk**.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
3. Tap **Install**.

The app works fully offline, with no ads, no accounts and no data collected ([privacy policy](https://sahajbalgunde.com/little-lotus-privacy/)). A Google Play version is coming soon.

## What is in the app

- **Play:** choose an age group (1–6, 7–13, 14–18, 19–25, 26+) and a topic: any puja, event or chakra.
  - *Which word did Shri Mataji say?* Fill in the missing word.
  - *Put her words back in order.*
  - *At which puja did she say this?* (ages 14 and up)
  - *In which decade did she say this?* (adults)
  - Little ones get two big choices, short sentences and read-aloud.
- **Talks:** more than 2,200 talks, grouped by puja, event, chakra and language, and searchable.
- **Print:** worksheets for any topic and age: colour the chakras, trace her words, multiple choice with QR codes, matching, word search, crossword, fill in the blanks, reflect and write, with an answer key.
- **Languages:** English, हिंदी (Hindi) and मराठी (Marathi).

Her words are never changed, and no AI wrote any of the text.

## Use it on a computer

Open `app/index.html` in Firefox or Brave. Everything works offline, including printing. The `app/` folder is also a complete static website that can be installed as a web app (PWA).
